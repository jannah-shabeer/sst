Preventive Methods for Students

Direct actions students take to reduce urban pollution.

Conserve Energy

Turn off lights, electronics, and cooling systems when leaving a room, and optimize appliance usage to reduce carbon emissions from power plants.

Plant Trees

Actively participate in greening school and community spaces to enhance carbon sequestration, improve air quality, and restore natural habitats.

Use Reusable Items

Substitute single-use plastics, water bottles, and stationery with durable, reusable alternatives to minimize landfill waste and production footprints.

Recycle Properly

Sort paper, plastics, glass, and metals into designated recycling streams to ensure materials are recovered and processed effectively.

Sustainable Transport

Walk, cycle, or use public transport and school buses instead of private vehicles to decrease daily vehicular emissions and noise pollution.

Raise Eco-Awareness & Report Waste

Educate peers about industrial pollution hazards, participate in eco-clubs, and report illegal dumping or industrial waste discharge to local authorities.

Causes & Impacts of Pollution

Overview of the 4 primary pollution streams.

1. Air Pollution (Toxic Gas)

Causes: Unfiltered factory stacks, heavy traffic exhaust, power plant fuel burning.

Impacts: Smog formation, acid rain, respiratory issues in living populations.

2. Water Pollution (Chemical Waste)

Causes: Untreated chemical discharge, plastic litter, toxic dumping into rivers.

Impacts: Destruction of marine life, unsafe drinking water, toxic accumulation in food chains.

3. Thermal Pollution (Hot Water)

Causes: High-temperature cooling water released directly into aquatic ecosystems.

Impacts: Rapid drop in dissolved oxygen, thermal shock, widespread fish kills.

4. Noise Pollution (Outdated Engine)

Causes: Unserviced factory machinery, generators, heavy diesel engines.

Impacts: Chronic stress, hearing loss, displacement of local bird and wildlife communication.
